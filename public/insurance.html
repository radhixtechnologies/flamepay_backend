    FAQ

    Insurance questions

    More answers in our Help Center.

    Who issues the policy?

    Policies are issued by the insurer you choose. FlamePay helps you compare and buy through authorised intermediaries.

    Does FlamePay charge extra?

    No. You pay only the premium set by the insurer.

    Can I cancel my policy?

    Yes, within the free-look period in your policy document, as per the insurer's terms.

    Who settles claims?

    Claims are settled by the insurer. Our team helps you raise and track them.
