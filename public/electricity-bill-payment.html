On this page

How to pay your electricity bill on FlamePay

  • Open Bill Payment → Electricity in the FlamePay app.
  • Choose your state and board from the list above.
  • Enter your consumer number — FlamePay fetches the amount due and due date from the board.
  • Pay by UPI, card or net banking and get an instant confirmation and receipt.

Where to find your consumer number

Your consumer number (also called account number, CA number, service number or K number depending on the board) is printed near the top of your electricity bill, usually next to your name and address. You can also find it in your board's SMS or email bill. Some boards need an extra detail such as a billing unit or sub-division — FlamePay shows the exact fields your board needs.

Why pay electricity bills on FlamePay

  • All boards in one app — pay your home bill, your parents' bill in another state and your shop's bill from one place.
  • Pay before the due date — avoid late-payment charges and disconnection notices.
  • Receipts saved — every payment has a reference number in your history, useful for rent, tax or office claims.
  • Auto-refund — if a payment fails after your account is debited, the amount is reversed automatically.

Tips to keep your bill low

  • Compare your units consumed month to month — sudden jumps can mean a faulty appliance or meter.
  • Check whether your board offers a prompt-payment or digital-payment rebate.
  • If your state offers a subsidy slab, make sure your connection category on the bill is correct.
  • Report a wrong meter reading to your board before paying, if the bill looks unusual.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take for the board to update my payment?

You get an instant confirmation in FlamePay. Most boards update their records within 1–3 working days.

Is there a fee to pay my electricity bill on FlamePay?

FlamePay charges ₹0 on most electricity bill payments. If a biller or payment method carries a fee, it is shown before you pay. See Pricing & Fees.

I paid the wrong consumer number. What now?

Contact your electricity board with the receipt from FlamePay — most boards can adjust the amount. Our support team can share the transaction details you need.

My payment failed but money was debited.

The amount is reversed automatically to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days. Read our Refund Policy.
