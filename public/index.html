FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know about FlamePay. Can't find your answer? Our team is here 24/7.

Talk to supportWe usually reply within minutes
Is FlamePay free to use?

Yes. Downloading FlamePay and creating an account is free. If any service carries a convenience fee, it is shown clearly before you confirm the payment.

What happens if my recharge or bill payment fails?

If money is debited but the transaction fails, it is automatically reversed to your original payment source, usually within 5–7 working days. You can track the status in the app. See our Refund Policy.

Does checking my CIBIL score on FlamePay reduce it?

No. Checking your own credit score is a soft enquiry and does not affect your score.

Does FlamePay give loans or credit cards itself?

No. FlamePay helps you compare and apply. Loans and credit cards are offered by partner banks and NBFCs, who decide approval, interest rate and terms.

Is my personal data safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and shared only with the partners needed to complete a service you request. We do not sell your personal data. Read our Privacy Policy.

How do I raise a complaint?

Use Help & Support in the app or write to us from the Contact page. If unresolved, you can escalate through our Grievance Redressal process.
